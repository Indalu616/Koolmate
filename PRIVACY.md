# KoolMate privacy policy

_Last updated: 23 November 2026_

KoolMate is a desktop app that files your downloads into course folders, and **KoolMate for Blackboard** is its companion browser extension. Both are built so that your files and your browsing stay on your own computer.

## What the browser extension does

When you open a course in Blackboard, the extension reads that course's name (for example "Human Computer Interaction") and Blackboard's internal course number. When you download a file from Blackboard, it passes the file's name and that course name to the KoolMate desktop app.

## Where that information goes

- It is sent **only to the KoolMate app on the same computer**, at the local address `127.0.0.1`. That address cannot be reached from the internet.
- It is **never sent to us or to any other server**. We have no servers that receive it.
- The extension keeps a short list of the Blackboard courses you have opened, in your browser's own extension storage, so it can match downloads to courses. Removing the extension deletes that list.
- The extension does **not** read your grades, messages, assignments, passwords or any page other than Blackboard course pages, and it does not track the other websites you visit.

## What the desktop app stores

The app keeps its settings, the list of files it has moved (so you can undo a move), and the courses it has learned in its own folder on your computer. File contents are read on your computer to work out which course a file belongs to.

The app has one **optional** online feature: if you add your own Google Gemini API key in Settings, the app can send a file's name and, only if you allow it, a short excerpt of the file to Google to improve its suggestions. This is off unless you turn it on. Google's own privacy policy applies to that request.

## Sharing and selling

We do not sell, share or transfer your information to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising or credit decisions.
